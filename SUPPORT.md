# Support

For general questions, use [yesid.dev/contact](https://yesid.dev/contact) or
email `contact@yesid.dev`.

Public issues may be used for reproducible defects in the dashboard or its
published data. Include the URL, browser and operating system, expected result,
actual result, and reproduction steps. Questions about an agency's schedules or
service belong with that agency.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
